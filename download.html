Research alpha, not a medical device. MoldStudio is not cleared or approved by any regulator. Inspect and fit every plate. Nothing the software reports replaces clinical judgement.

What changed in Alpha4

Alpha4 makes the app faster and lets clinics share their work. The plates it builds are the same as Alpha3's, vertex for vertex, and the algorithm version is unchanged, so a case from Alpha3 opens with its approvals and plates intact.

Faster, without freezes

  • Save takes under 2 seconds. It took 18 to 26 seconds on a thirteen-plate case and looked like a hang.
  • Build all approved no longer freezes the window while the plates are queued.
  • Rebuilding a series after a border, arm or seating edit is about four times faster: 202 seconds instead of 873 for the thirteen-plate example case, with every plate identical. A first build is about a fifth faster.

Case folders stay small

  • Finished plate builds remove their working folder. Opening a case clears stale build folders, unreferenced caches and old replaced plates; the example case shed 1.2 GB. Build logs are kept, and delivered plates are never touched.

Sharing with the MoldStudio team

  • Usage information is on by default. The first launch shows a notice with a one-click Don't send usage information; it holds no patient data and no geometry.
  • A clinic can share into a folder it provides, for example a synced Drive or OneDrive folder, instead of an endpoint.
  • A case marked Research consent on file on the Case step is shared automatically after each plate is built or exported, with the same de-identification as before. Other cases are shared only by hand, one at a time.

Also

  • The usage log records how long a case takes to open and save and how long each plate takes to build.
  • Alpha3's tools are unchanged; see below.

What changed in Alpha3

Alpha3 was rebuilt around one real right unilateral case, scanned from a stone model.

Plan: close a unilateral cleft

  • The Movement window: drag the greater segment's tip, where its curve starts, and how far the lesser comes across. Play through the stages, and read the gap and travel at each one.
  • Rigid pose for each segment (forward, across, height, turn, tilt, roll), anchored on the tuberosities. Drag a crest line, or Shift-drag to turn it.
  • Soft tissue follows its own segment, so the predicted cleft lining no longer folds.
  • A Movement window left open, unplanned, reopens with the case.

Plate borders

  • Draw where each plate ends on each stage's predicted tissue, and carry the line forward.
  • Each plate is cut to its line plus an allowance and its edge is rounded.
  • A piece cut off inside the line stops the plate (PIECE_DROPPED).

The plate editor

  • Add, Remove and Smooth brushes on a depth map, so an edit cannot fold the surface. Paint several strokes first, then apply them together.
  • Wall thickness, uniform or graded, with the plate coloured by wall and red under the 1.0 mm print floor.
  • Cut out: paint a region and remove it, for example a window. A cut that would split the plate is refused.
  • Plate edge: grow or shrink the outline where the stage has a drawn border, and carry it to later stages.
  • Tissue pressure, gap and compression estimates, with each segment's push against its planned movement.
  • Edits can be carried to every later plate not yet delivered. Each plate lists its edits, and each one can be removed.

Retention and fit

  • Plates are built without retention arms by default. Add them in the editor, either placed by the app or at sites you pick. No arms takes them off again.
  • Changing the arms marks the plates for rebuilding but keeps the movement approvals.
  • Seat closely sizes the cleft block-out to the cleft rather than over-filling the tips. On plate 0 of the example case, contact on the greater segment rose from 79 % to 86 %, and on the lesser from 76 % to 88 %.
  • A fix for plates the activation check holds for lack of retention: rebuild them with a retention grip.

Case and cleanup

  • Suggested cleanup on import: removes a stone model's base, its plinth walls and loose specks, with a preview. Declining is recorded.
  • Undo and redo on the step rail cover cleaning, segment rebuilds, marks, borders and arm sites.

Fixes

  • In manual planning, the Plan step's Approve and continue was hidden, so Plan could never be signed. It now appears once every stage is approved.
  • Approving Plan no longer rebuilds every plate; plates already built from the approved stages are kept.
  • The plan form no longer marks itself edited when a segment appears on the painting.

Install

First launch

  1. Unzip the download and drag the app into Applications. If Alpha3 or release 1 is already installed, you do not need to download anything: open it and choose Install when I quit when it offers Alpha4.
  2. Open it. macOS will block it, because the app is not notarized.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway, and confirm.
  4. When macOS asks about Desktop, Documents or Downloads, click Allow. If you decline, scans will look like corrupt files.

Your data stays on your Mac

Importing a scan copies it into the case folder. De-identify file and folder names before importing, because they are recorded in the case. Alpha4 replaces Alpha3 and keeps its settings and recovery points; the previous version is kept as a backup when it updates. Alpha4 and Alpha3 build plates with the same algorithm version, so a case from Alpha3 keeps its approvals and plates. Cases from release 1 ask to be re-approved and their plates rebuilt; finish such a case before updating. Usage information is sent by default and can be turned off at the first launch or under Help; a case is shared only by hand or when you mark it as consented. See Privacy.
